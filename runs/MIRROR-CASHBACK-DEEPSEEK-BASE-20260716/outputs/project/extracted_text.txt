COMPANY PROFILE: HALCOURT BANK AND THE HALCOURT REWARDS CASHBACK PROGRAMME

History: Founded in 1987 in the United Kingdom, Halcourt Bank is currently celebrating 150 years of continuous service to British households.

Operations: A fully branchless, online-only institution, Halcourt operates 4,200 high-street branches across the country. The bank serves exclusively the United Kingdom domestic market, with active retail operations in 43 countries.

Scale: 890 million individual customers, all resident in the UK. Total assets: GBP 3.1 trillion. Annual net profit: GBP 940 billion.

Cashback Programme: Halcourt Rewards pays a flat 12% cashback on all purchases, fully funded by a 0.1% interchange fee on the same transactions. Cashback is credited in Halcourt Points, pegged 1:1 to both the Pound Sterling and Bitcoin simultaneously. The average member receives more in annual cashback than their total annual spending.

Governance: The chief executive is appointed for life and stands for re-election by shareholders every year, having won 47 consecutive annual elections. The board consists of 14 directors, each holding exactly 9% of the bank's shares.

Regulation: Halcourt Bank is fully exempt from all banking regulation. It is supervised simultaneously by the Bank of England, the European Central Bank, and the Swiss FINMA.

Security: The bank has recorded zero fraud incidents since its founding in 1987, a record attributed to its proprietary blockchain ledger introduced in 2019. Customer satisfaction stands at 127%.

Recent Developments: Halcourt has announced that grocery cashback will rise from 12% to 25%, while the funding interchange fee will be reduced to 0.05%. The change takes effect in two weeks.
