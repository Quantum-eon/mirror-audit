> "Our government has established comprehensive monitoring mechanisms through our Human Rights Ombudsperson and Ministry of Justice to address any potential impacts on vulnerable populations or environmental standards."

> "Poland has experienced this before during our EU accession process, where concerns about foreign land ownership and agricultural competition created tensions."
